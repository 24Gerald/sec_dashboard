# Web Platform Vulnerability Assessment — Report

**Engagement:** ARK-E-2025-0001 · **Date:** 22 September 2025 · **Author:** Amara Okonkwo

## Summary
A two-week authenticated and unauthenticated vulnerability assessment of the ARK Studios web platform ahead of launch. The assessment covered the OWASP Top 10, authentication and session handling, access control and common misconfigurations across 128 endpoints and 14 hosts.

## Key findings
| Severity | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | SQL injection in product search (`ARK-F-2025-0001`) | Fixed & verified |
| Medium | Session cookie missing HttpOnly/Secure (`ARK-F-2026-0005`) | Fixed |

## Highlights
- The critical SQL injection was identified and **fixed before launch**, preventing a full database compromise.
- Session cookie flags and a Content-Security-Policy were hardened.
- A monthly re-scan cadence and Semgrep PR checks were established.

## Recommendations
1. Adopt parameterised queries everywhere (enforced by CI).
2. Harden all cookies and security headers by default.
3. Continue monthly automated scanning with manual verification.
