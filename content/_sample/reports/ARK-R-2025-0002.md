# Staking Vault Smart Contract Audit — Report

**Engagement:** ARK-E-2025-0002 · **Date:** 27 October 2025 · **Author:** Wei Zhang

## Scope
`StakingVault.sol`, `RewardDistributor.sol`, `ARKToken.sol` — 1,840 lines of Solidity reviewed prior to mainnet deployment, with a 42-test Foundry suite.

## Findings
| Severity | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Reentrancy in `withdraw()` (`ARK-F-2025-0007`) | Fixed & verified |
| High | Missing access control on `setRewardRate()` (`ARK-F-2025-0008`) | Fixed |

## Critical: Reentrancy
`withdraw()` transferred funds **before** updating balances, allowing a malicious contract to recursively re-enter and drain the vault in a single transaction. A Foundry PoC drained the full 50 ETH test balance.

**Remediation:** Checks-Effects-Interactions ordering plus a `nonReentrant` guard. An invariant test now asserts `sum(balances) == address(this).balance`.

## Outcome
Both issues were fixed and re-tested before deployment. The audit harness is now reused across all contract audits.
